# SystemSculpt 6.18.17

## Send feedback without leaving Obsidian

Choose **Send feedback** in SystemSculpt settings or the chat's **More** menu.
Both open the same form: one message, sent only when you choose **Send feedback**.
No reminders, feedback popups or extra questions.

Your message is queued as an email to `support@systemsculpt.com`, with your plugin
version and your account when signed in. Notes, chats and diagnostics are not attached.
You can also send feedback without signing in. If sending fails, your draft stays
in the form so you can try again.

To update, open **Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates**, then choose
**Update** beside SystemSculpt AI.
