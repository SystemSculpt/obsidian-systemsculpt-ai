# SystemSculpt 6.18.20

## Your first answer, one tap after sign-in

After you sign in, **Summarize my latest note** runs on your free credits and opens the answer in a new chat. It uses the note you have open, or your most recently edited one. It skips SystemSculpt's own chats and recordings, templates, drawings, excluded files, and notes that are nearly empty or very large. A vault with no notes yet gets **Start a chat** instead.

## Out of credits, one tap to Monthly

When your free credits run out, **Subscribe** in the chat and the **Out of credits** label below it open the $9 Monthly checkout directly. An empty chat no longer offers the free start you already used. Accounts with a paid plan still see their plans.

To update, open **Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates**, then choose **Update** beside SystemSculpt AI.
