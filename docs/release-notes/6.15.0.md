# SystemSculpt 6.15.0

A redesigned chat, from the first prompt to the last change: clearer conversations, compact controls, and a keyboard-first composer.

- **Read the answer, not the chrome.** Your messages sit in compact bubbles; assistant replies use full-width Obsidian Markdown. Tool activity folds into readable lines you can expand for commands, results and diffs. Finished turns summarize the notes they changed. Declined actions are labeled **Denied**, not failed.
- **Know what runs where.** The run line below the message box shows your agent, permission mode and model—or your hosted credits. Change any part directly without opening a settings dialog. Auto mode and your own Claude Code or Codex continue to work as introduced in 6.14.0.
- **Keep your conversations close.** Search and resume chats inside the view with Cmd/Ctrl+K. Rename the title in place, favorite a chat, or open it in another tab. Switching agents starts a new chat, carries your draft and attachments, and leaves a link back to the previous conversation.
- **Bring the right context.** Type `@` or `[[` to attach a vault note, use the **+** menu, paste an image, or drop files into the chat. Attachment chips let you open, remove or pin context for every message. An active-note suggestion adds nothing until you choose it.
- **Stay on the keyboard.** Enter sends on desktop, Shift+Enter adds a line, and Escape stops a running response after closing any open menu. Queue a follow-up while the agent works. Inline approval cards show the proposed change: Enter approves, Escape denies, and Alt+Up brings a pending card into focus without interrupting your draft.
- **Comfortable in a sidebar or a full tab.** Updated light and dark styling, visible keyboard focus, clearer contrast, touch-sized controls, and reduced-motion support adapt to the space you have. Mobile Enter adds a newline; use Send to submit.
- **Keep meeting captures organized.** Meeting captures from your iPhone save to **SystemSculpt/Meetings**, with summaries, decisions, checkable action items and a folded transcript. Choose another Meetings folder in **Settings → SystemSculpt AI → Phone captures**. Ordinary notes and transcript-only captures continue to use your inbox folder.

Saved chats retain their backend and history. Your native agents still own their credentials, permissions and sessions. Session approval is not a blanket permission bypass. Mobile uses hosted SystemSculpt.

Update through **Obsidian → Settings → Community plugins → Check for updates**.
