# SystemSculpt 6.19.1

## Search marks the words that matter

**Search** now highlights the words your question is about, at the start of a word. It no longer marks every "the", "is" or "at" in a result, or letters inside other words. A question whose words match no note waits for smart search before saying there are no matches.

## Search and chat stay put while you type

Clearing **Search** shows your recent notes at once, not the old results for a moment. In chat, the current-note chip fades as you start typing instead of vanishing and jumping the message box.

## Studio cards show one view of their text

Text cards in **Studio** show either **Raw** or **Rendered** again, not both at once, and note cards no longer show a mode switch they don't use.

## Generated images sit inside their Outputs frame

When a new image finishes loading on the canvas, its **Outputs** frame and label now grow to fit it, instead of the label landing on top of the image. The image appears in the same moment, not as a blank card first.

## Studio tools fit on a narrow screen

On a phone or a narrow pane, **Studio**'s nine drawing tools share one row instead of leaving the arrow alone on a row of its own.

## Search results read as plain text

**Search** previews show a note's words, not its Markdown: no `#`, `[[links]]`, task boxes or callout syntax, and an excerpt never cuts a word in half. The search box no longer draws a second box and focus ring inside the search bar, and the list fades at the bottom only while more results sit below.

## Edits read in full on a narrow pane

In a narrow chat pane, an edit's diff wraps long lines instead of hiding their ends behind a sideways scroll, and an added line's text lines up with the lines around it.

## Narrow Studio cards keep their names

A **Studio** card resized narrow puts its kind on its own line, so its name stays readable instead of shrinking to a sliver. A text card's text fills the card instead of stopping above a band of empty space, and the **Run** button's green meets text contrast.
