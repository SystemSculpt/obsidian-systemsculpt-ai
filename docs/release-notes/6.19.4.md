# SystemSculpt 6.19.4

## Search previews end on a whole word

A **Search** preview that runs past its line now ends on the last whole word that fits, then an ellipsis, instead of a word cut in half. It fits again when the window changes width.

## Studio says what it is generating

While an image or video generates, **Studio** shows "Generating image…" as plain, readable text above the frame, instead of words drawn over a loading bar.

## Narrow diffs and text cards line up

In a narrow chat pane, a wrapped diff line splits evenly instead of leaving one word alone on the next line. **Studio** text cards line their text up with the card's title and settings.
