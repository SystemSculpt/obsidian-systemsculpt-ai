# SystemSculpt 6.18.25

## One header in Chat

Chat now shows its title once, in Obsidian's own header. **Chat history** and **New chat** sit beside it, and the one **More options** menu holds Rename chat, Text size, Export and settings. On desktop and phone.

In a sidebar, or with Obsidian's tab title bar turned off, Chat keeps its own compact title row so those controls stay one tap away.

Primary buttons use a deeper accent, so their labels are easier to read in light themes.
