# SystemSculpt 6.18.30

## Search marks the words that matter

**Search** now highlights the words your question is about, at the start of a word. It no longer marks every "the", "is" or "at" in a result, or letters inside other words.

## Studio cards show one view of their text

Text cards in **Studio** show either **Raw** or **Rendered** again, not both at once, and note cards no longer show a mode switch they don't use.

## Generated images sit inside their Outputs frame

When a new image finishes loading on the canvas, its **Outputs** frame and label now grow to fit it, instead of the label landing on top of the image.
