# SystemSculpt 6.18.23

## Pro when you choose hosted Chat

Free starter accounts now see **Get Pro for $9/month** above the hosted Chat composer, including with all 500 starter credits left. The link opens Monthly checkout directly. Your free credits remain usable; no upgrade is required to keep using them.

The offer stays hidden for native Claude Code and Codex, paid plans, and unverified account status. Checkout checks your plan again before opening. Failed renewals still lead to **Update your card**, not a second subscription.

Account settings and out-of-credit actions keep their existing behavior.
